Task 3 – Causality Assessment

Objective

To evaluate the relationship between a drug and an adverse drug reaction using standard causality assessment methods and classify the reaction as Certain, Probable/Likely, or Possible.

Case Analysis

Patient: Ramesh Patel
Age: 32 years
Gender: Male
Diagnosis: Acute Bacterial Sinusitis

Suspected Drug: Amoxicillin 500 mg TID
Treatment started: 1 March 2026

Adverse Reaction: Generalized itching and skin rashes over the chest
ADR onset: 3 March 2026

Action Taken: Amoxicillin was stopped on 3 March 2026. Antihistaminics were given.

Outcome: Complete recovery by 6 March 2026.

Causality Assessment

Classification: Probable/Likely

Reason

The adverse reaction occurred after initiation of Amoxicillin and the patient recovered after the suspected drug was discontinued. Rechallenge was not reported.

Classification Criteria

- Certain: Strong temporal relationship, clear dechallenge/rechallenge and no reasonable alternative cause.
- Probable/Likely: Reasonable temporal relationship, positive dechallenge and alternative causes are unlikely.
- Possible: Reasonable temporal relationship, but other causes may also explain the reaction.
